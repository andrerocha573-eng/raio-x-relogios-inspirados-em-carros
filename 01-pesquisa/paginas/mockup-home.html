Our Story

Why we built Corsa Storica

The golden age of European motorsport — the Mille Miglia's thousand miles through Italy, Le Mans' 24 hours of endurance, Targa Florio's mountain circuit — shaped the cars we still admire today. That heritage is usually locked behind luxury watch prices.

We design each piece as a small archive of one race, with the same rotating dial mechanism watch collectors already trust — priced for people who love the story, not just the status.
